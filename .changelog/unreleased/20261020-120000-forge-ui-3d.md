forge-ui now draws your runs as a 3D map in archivision's style. The front page is a map of every run, grouped by repo, with a lamp per task and filters (state, repo, kind, model, age, review verdict) that fade what does not match. A run shows its tasks in their planned waves, with dependency cables, ending in three finish nodes: integration, verification and the whole-run final review. Switch to *Execution* to see the same run on wall-clock time: one block per attempt in the lane it actually ran in, with the attempts that have not started yet projected, and a strip of busy slots against `--max-parallel`. Click a task to open it to its attempts, dwarves, reviews and escalations; the inspector beside it shows the exact prompt that launched each step, the model, the timings by phase, tokens, the verdict and every P0–P3 finding, the final review's included. Statuses update live while a run works, and you can replay a run, light its critical path, and size or colour tasks by duration, tokens, attempts or model. The 3D view loads three.js from cdn.jsdelivr.net the first time it is shown; offline, or with *2D* chosen, the page works without it. The 2D dependency graph shows its status colours again (the page's own security policy had been blocking them).
