`plan` now explores by default (`--explore 0.2`): now and then, a row left to forge gets the model one tier away in your own pools (cheaper for medium and high tasks, stronger for low ones), with the odds recorded. The new `forge dataset routing` command compares each pair of models from those draws, and also shows every model's first-pass rate and runtime per tier. `--explore 0` turns exploration off, and that choice is kept for later re-plans.
