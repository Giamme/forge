`forge control steer <plan> <task> "text"` queues a message for a task's next implementer attempt, on a live run or not. It starts nothing: the next attempt, whatever starts it, gets the message in its prompt beside any review findings, and its reviewer sees it as part of the requirements. forge-ui has a *Message for the next attempt* box on each task and marks steered attempts on the attempt trail. Steered attempts are recorded in the dataset (`steered`: actor, time, hash and length) and left out of `forge dataset routing`, `reviewers`, `pairs`, `export` and forge-ui's leaderboard unless `--include-steered` is given (#134).
