`sol` and `astra` now resolve to gpt-6.2-sol and gpt-6.2-astra on codex, openclaude and opencode. The previous models are still available as `sol-6.1` and `astra-6`, and dispatches recorded on them are counted under those names in `forge dataset reviewers`, review history and forge-ui's model leaderboard, never mixed into the new `sol` and `astra`. A pin such as `astra:xhigh` from forge-ui now runs the 6.2 model instead of silently falling back to the older one.
