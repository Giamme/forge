Jev no longer suggests efforts or rewrites tiers, because forge's run history showed it could not choose either: its effort suggestions carried no information beyond the task's tier. `forge jev calibrate`, `forge jev parse-spec` and `forge jev qa-effort` are gone, and so are the effort suggestions in Jev's routing lines and Fractal's re-rating of child tasks. `--jev-act` is still accepted but changes nothing. `forge jev doctor` lists the `routing_act` and `calibrate_floor` thresholds as safe to delete from an existing config. Drift checks, memory curation, verification and the other advisory gates are unchanged.
